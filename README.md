# WorkerInformedAI

**Meaningful worker participation in AI and workplace technology.**

This repository provides open-source resources to embed worker-informed participation into the design, procurement, modification, and governance of AI/software systems — especially those affecting health, safety, and working conditions.

## Core Resource: Worker-Informed Participation Protocol (WIPP)

WIPP is a practical, adaptable protocol that moves beyond simple consultation to **active co-participation** with substantive worker voice.

[Read the full WIPP Boilerplate Clause →](WIPP-CLAUSE.md)

## Key Features
- Ready-to-adapt contract and policy language ([WIPP Clause](WIPP-CLAUSE.md))
- Templates for joint impact assessments and governance ([Impact Assessment Template](impact-assessment-template.md))
- Guidance for participatory design workshops
- **Reference architecture** for collaborative policy authoring, per-request enforcement through existing AI gateways with Open Policy Agent, and a hash-chained OpenTelemetry audit stream for the Joint Committee ([docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md))
- **Worked walkthrough**, an illustrative example taking a CBA clause to a WIPP term, worker notice, Rego rule, and audit event ([examples/cba-to-policy-walkthrough.md](examples/cba-to-policy-walkthrough.md))
- **Vendor Telemetry Addendum** with clause language for vendor data disclosure and log export ([clauses/vendor-telemetry-addendum.md](clauses/vendor-telemetry-addendum.md))
- **Sector version: Construction & Field Services**, covering multi-employer sites, wearables, scheduling and dispatch, and exposure monitoring ([clauses/WIPP-CLAUSE-construction-field-services.md](clauses/WIPP-CLAUSE-construction-field-services.md))

See the [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) for what is in development.

## Why This Matters
Workers closest to the work possess unique insight into real-world risks and potential harms. WIPP helps organizations meet NIST AI RMF, DOL AI Principles, and union best practices while reducing deployment risks and improving outcomes.

## Pilots & Partners
We invite worker organizations, unions, EHS teams, and labor centers to pilot WIPP, including the clause, the sector versions, or the reference architecture, and to share what works and what doesn't. To start, [open an issue](https://github.com/nphoenix/WorkerInformedAI/issues) describing your context. Please don't include confidential or personal information.
