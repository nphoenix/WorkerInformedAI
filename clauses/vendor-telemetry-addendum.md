# WIPP Vendor Telemetry & Data Disclosure Addendum

**Version 1.0**  
**Date:** October 2026  
**Companion to:** [WIPP Clause](../WIPP-CLAUSE.md) · [Reference Architecture, Part 4](../docs/architecture.md#4-embedded-vendor-telemetry)

## Purpose

Many workplace software products collect usage analytics and telemetry, sometimes linked to identifiable workers, and send it directly to the vendor's systems. This traffic often bypasses the employer's own controls. This addendum gives the employer and the Joint Worker Participation Committee contractual visibility into that data.

It is meant to be attached to a procurement contract, master services agreement, or data processing agreement. Text in **[brackets]** is to be completed or chosen by the parties. This is template language, not legal advice. Have it reviewed by qualified counsel for your jurisdiction.

---

## Addendum Language

### 1. Definitions
1.1 **"Telemetry"** means any data generated by or collected through the Product about its use, performance, environment, or users, and sent to Vendor or any third party. It includes analytics events, crash reports, session recordings, device identifiers, and location data.

1.2 **"Worker Data"** means Telemetry or other data that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked to an individual worker, including pseudonymous identifiers.

1.3 **"Committee"** means the Joint Worker Participation Committee established under the Customer's Worker-Informed Participation Protocol (WIPP).

### 2. Pre-Contract Data Inventory
2.1 Before contract execution, Vendor shall provide a written **Data Inventory** listing, for each category of Telemetry and Worker Data:
- (a) the data elements collected;
- (b) the purpose of collection;
- (c) destination domains, endpoints, and hosting regions;
- (d) sub-processors and third parties receiving the data;
- (e) retention period and deletion method; and
- (f) whether the data is used to train or improve any AI or machine-learning model.

2.2 The Data Inventory shall be shared with the Committee and recorded in the WIPP Record. It forms part of this Agreement.

2.3 Vendor shall give **[30]** days' written notice before any material change to the Data Inventory, including new data categories, destinations, sub-processors, or AI training uses. Such changes are subject to Committee review under WIPP requirement (d).

### 3. Purpose Limitation
3.1 Vendor shall not use Worker Data for any purpose not listed in the Data Inventory, including advertising, sale or sharing with data brokers, or training AI models, unless the Customer has given written consent following Committee review.

3.2 Vendor shall not provide the Customer with individual-level worker monitoring, productivity scoring, or behavioral profiling derived from Telemetry unless that function is explicitly listed in the Data Inventory and approved under the Customer's WIPP process.

### 4. Configuration and Minimization
4.1 Vendor shall provide documented controls allowing the Customer to disable or minimize non-essential Telemetry, and to pseudonymize or aggregate Worker Data where technically feasible.

4.2 Defaults applied to the Customer's deployment shall be those agreed with the Committee and recorded in the WIPP Record.

### 5. Log Export
5.1 Vendor shall provide the Customer with an export of Telemetry transmission logs at least **[monthly]**, and within **[10]** business days of a written request. The export shall show the categories of data sent, destinations, volumes, and time periods.

5.2 Exports shall be in a documented, machine-readable format **[e.g. JSON Lines or OpenTelemetry-compatible]** suitable for ingestion into the Customer's audit log.

5.3 The Customer may share log exports and summaries with the Committee, subject to the confidentiality and privacy terms agreed with worker representatives.

### 6. Verification
6.1 Vendor acknowledges that the Customer may observe network metadata (such as DNS queries and destination addresses) generated by the Product, **without decrypting traffic content**, to verify consistency with the Data Inventory.

6.2 If observed destinations or volumes are materially inconsistent with the Data Inventory, Vendor shall provide a written explanation within **[15]** business days and, where the transmission is not covered by the Data Inventory, cease it or amend the Data Inventory through the change process in Section 2.3.

### 7. Worker Data-Access Requests
7.1 Vendor shall assist the Customer in responding to requests by workers to access, correct, or delete their Worker Data under applicable law (for example, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, or the EU General Data Protection Regulation), within the time limits that law requires.

7.2 Where no statutory right applies, Vendor shall nonetheless provide the Customer, on request, a copy of the Worker Data held about an individual worker who has asked to see it, at no additional charge **[up to N requests per year]**.

7.3 Results of access requests may be summarized for the Committee only with the requesting worker's consent and with personal details removed.

### 8. Breach and Remedies
8.1 Undisclosed collection or use of Worker Data is a material breach of this Agreement.

8.2 On such a breach, the Customer may, in addition to other remedies, suspend use of the affected functionality, require deletion of the affected data with written certification, and **[terminate without penalty / apply service credits]**.

### 9. Order of Precedence
9.1 Where this Addendum conflicts with any other term of the Agreement on the collection or use of Worker Data, the term more protective of workers controls.

## Implementation Notes
- Use Section 2 at the procurement approval gate, and check the Data Inventory against observed egress after deployment (see the [reference architecture](../docs/architecture.md)).
- Vendor log exports in Section 5 can be appended to the same hash-chained audit log used for gateway decisions.
- Smaller customers may not be able to negotiate every section. Sections 2, 3, and 5 should take priority.
